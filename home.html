HLT/EMNLP 2005

Human Language Technology Conference
Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing

October 6-8, 2005
Vancouver, B.C., Canada

 

 

In 2005, HLT (Human Language Technology Conference) and EMNLP (Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing) will be a joint conference held in Vancouver, B.C., Canada.

The proceedings of the conference are available at the ACL anthology.

Best Student Paper Award

Ryan McDonald, Fernando Pereira, Kiril Ribarov and Jan Hajic. Non-Projective Dependency Parsing using Spanning Tree Algorithms.
The award includes a prize of $2000 from IBM.
